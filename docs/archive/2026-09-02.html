对 AI builder 而言，这条观点提醒我们警惕产品设计中的过度拟人化倾向。从工程角度，LLM 本质是统计模型，采样的输出常常被误解为"意图"或"人格"；若产品界面、话术设计围绕"AI 是人"来构建，会让用户形成错误心智模型，导致信任崩塌或过度依赖。从产品逻辑看，把 AI 定位成 utility（工具）更利于建立清晰的能力边界与免责预期——像对待 API、搜索引擎一样对待 AI。这与 Anysphere/Anthropic 坚持的"可靠性优先、避免自我声称情感"一致。对设计落地意味：UX 强调任务闭环而非人设聊天，合规与风险防范也更清晰。
For AI builders, this raises a pointed warning against over-anthropomorphism in product design. On the engineering side, LLMs are statistical models whose sampled outputs are frequently misread as "intent" or "personality"; an interface and copy built around "AI as a person" gives users a broken mental model, breeding either shattered trust or over-reliance. From a product logic standpoint, positioning AI as a utility clarifies capability boundaries and liability expectations, like treating it as an API or a search engine. On the ground, this means UX should emphasize task closure over persona chat, and compliance and risk mitigation become cleaner.

For AI builders this signals two things: agent toolchains are moving from text generation toward normalized deliverables (implying structured constraints and determinism); and "self-contained HTML + motion" hints at a template plus data-driven pipeline (custom DSL to SVG/Canvas), reusable for code review, automated tech docs, and downstream consumption of agent outputs.
