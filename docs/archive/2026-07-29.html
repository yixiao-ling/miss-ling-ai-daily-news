For AI builders, this is a direct collision between "formally verified code" and "AI-generated code." On the engineering side: traditional CSG rendering is plagued by floating-point imprecision and countless edge cases (e.g., tiny gaps between intersected solids, flickering at intersection pixels). AI code (Copilot, etc.) is fast but opaque and carries zero correctness guarantees. On the product side: for CAD, medical imaging, robot simulation – any domain where a 3D bug costs real money or safety – this enables a shift from test-driven to spec-driven rendering pipelines. Compared to OpenSCAD (traditional code) or NVIDIA's neural rendering (fast but unprovable), the trade-off is clear: high upfront spec-writing cost vs. absolute downstream reliability. Not for fast-moving consumer 3D tools, but a game-changer for mission-critical geometry.
