Algorithmically, formal verification offers a convenient RL reward signal, yet it mismatches how mathematicians actually work. Product-wise, a research assistant should prioritize literature retrieval, counterexample search, conjecture generation and proof-draft review rather than being a proof autocompleter. When selecting models or benchmarks, watch for Goodhart effects: high scores on proof tasks do not imply the system helps researchers produce genuinely new theorems.
