// 影响分析// Impact Analysis
对 AI builder 而言，这是一个信号：开源社区正在用规则而非技术手段对"AI 生成代码"做准入过滤。产品上，如果你的项目主打 LLM 辅助开发，依赖 Reddit/Discord 等社区做冷启动的渠道会明显收窄，需转向自有渠道或更宽容的平台（GitHub、Hugging Face、Discord 自建）。技术上，社区缺乏可验证的"LLM 参与度"检测手段，此类禁令执行几乎只能是主观判定，成本高、误伤大；反过来看，这意味着"代码来源可信度证明"（如 provenance、提交签名、AI 使用声明）可能成为新的工具机会。
For AI builders this is a signal that open-source communities are starting to gatekeep "AI-generated code" through policy rather than tooling. On the product side, if your project leans on LLM-assisted development, community channels like Reddit and Discord for cold start will narrow, pushing you toward GitHub, Hugging Face, or owned channels. Technically, there is no reliable way to detect LLM involvement in code, so such bans are enforced by subjective judgment at high cost and with heavy false positives. Conversely, this opens a tooling opportunity around provenance, commit signing, and AI-usage disclosure that lets projects prove how their code was produced.
