从工程角度看，这条经验的核心是：eBPF 程序运行在内核态、每包/每事件都触发，单次开销被高频放大，因此对纯函数式的查表、解析、统计类逻辑做记忆化（key→结果缓存）常能带来数量级收益，而不必更换硬件或重写数据面。产品视角上，这意味着可观测性 agent、服务网格、容器网络方案可以用更低 CPU 占用换取更高采样率与更细监控粒度，降低客户云成本，成为同类产品（如各类 eBPF 探针、网络策略引擎）的差异化卖点。需注意缓存失效、状态爆炸与并发安全，避免"缓存本身"成为新瓶颈。
Engineering-wise, eBPF programs run in kernel space and fire on every packet or event, so per-invocation cost is amplified by frequency. For pure-function logic like lookups, parsing, and counting, memoizing key-to-result mappings can yield order-of-magnitude gains without new hardware or a data-plane rewrite. Product-wise, observability agents, service meshes, and container networking can trade lower CPU for higher sampling rates and finer monitoring granularity, reducing customer cloud bills and becoming a differentiator versus competing eBPF probes. Watch out for cache invalidation, state explosion, and concurrency safety—otherwise the cache itself becomes the new bottleneck.
